If you've just joined B.Tech Computer Science at a KTU college, this is your map for
**Semester 1 under the 2024 scheme** — the subjects, how credits are split, and where
to get notes for each.

## KTU CSE Semester 1 subjects

Most colleges run a **Physics cycle** and a **Chemistry cycle** in the first year.
Your Semester 1 set usually includes:

- **Mathematics** (calculus / linear algebra foundations)
- **Physics or Chemistry** (depending on your cycle)
- An **engineering core** course
- **Programming / computing fundamentals**
- **Lab** courses
- **Humanities / audit** course

> Always confirm against your college's official timetable — cycles can be swapped
> between Semester 1 and Semester 2.

## How credits are distributed

Semester 1 is typically **~18–21 credits**. Theory subjects carry the most weight,
with labs and audit courses making up the rest. Because the 2024 scheme adds weight
to internal assessment, lab and assignment marks matter from week one.

KTU first year is **mostly common across all branches** — the same foundation of maths,
science, and basic engineering before you specialise from Semester 3. Here's the layout.

## Physics cycle vs Chemistry cycle

Colleges divide first-year students into two groups:

- One group does the **Physics cycle** in Semester 1, **Chemistry cycle** in Semester 2.
- The other group does the **reverse**.

By the end of first year, everyone has covered the same subjects — only the order differs.

## Common first year subjects

- **Mathematics** (two courses across the year)
- **Physics** and **Chemistry** (one per semester per cycle)
- A **basic engineering** course (electrical, mechanical, civil or graphics)
- **Programming / computing fundamentals**
- **Labs** (science + engineering)
- **Humanities / life-skills / audit** courses

## How to study first year well

1. Don't underestimate **maths** — it underpins later semesters.
2. Keep lab records up to date; they carry internal marks.
3. Use [module-wise notes](/notes) and solve PYQs for each subject.
4. Pick your branch landing page (e.g. [CSE](/cs/sem-1)) for targeted resources.

## Quick answer

**First year = common foundation (maths, physics, chemistry, basic engineering,
programming, labs), split into Physics and Chemistry cycles.** Branch-specific
subjects start in Semester 3.

If a KTU result looks lower than you expected, you have two options: **rechecking**
and **revaluation**. Here's how each works and how to apply.

## Rechecking vs revaluation

- **Rechecking** — an official confirms every answer was evaluated and the marks
  were added up correctly. It does *not* re-judge your answers. Cheaper, faster.
- **Revaluation** — your answer script is re-evaluated by an examiner. This can
  raise (or occasionally lower) your marks. Costs more per subject.

## How to apply (step by step)

1. Wait for results and the **revaluation window** announcement.
2. Log in to your official **KTU student portal**.
3. Select the subject(s) you want rechecked or revalued.
4. Pay the **per-subject fee** online.
5. Submit before the **deadline** — late requests are not accepted.
6. Track the portal for the revaluation result.

> Fees and deadlines change each exam session. Always follow the official KTU
> notification for exact amounts and dates.

## Should you apply?

Consider revaluation if you scored close to the next grade boundary or far below
your internal performance. Pull the question paper and your
[module-wise notes](/notes) to estimate how many marks you may have lost.

## Quick answer

**Rechecking = recount only. Revaluation = fresh evaluation.** Apply through the
official KTU portal within the deadline and pay the per-subject fee.

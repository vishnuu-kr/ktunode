Failing a subject isn't the end — KTU runs **supplementary (supply) exams** so you can
clear backlogs and stay on track. Here's how they work.

## Who is eligible

You're eligible for a supplementary exam if you **failed** or were **absent** for the
regular university exam of a course. Supplementary exams are typically held alongside
the next regular exam session.

## How to register

1. Watch for the **supplementary registration window** on the KTU portal.
2. Log in and select your **backlog subject(s)**.
3. Pay the **per-subject fee** online.
4. Submit before the **deadline** and download your hall ticket when released.

## Do your internals carry over?

In most cases, **yes** — your earlier internal/sessional marks are retained, so you
only need to re-sit the external university exam. Confirm with the current regulations.

## How to actually clear it

Backlog subjects fail for a reason, so don't repeat the same prep:

1. Pull the **previous year papers** and identify high-mark, repeated topics.
2. Study those modules first using clean [module-wise notes](/notes).
3. Solve at least 3 past papers under timed conditions.
4. Revise one-page summaries the day before.

See our full [7-step KTU exam plan](/blog/how-to-prepare-for-ktu-exams) for the method.

## Quick answer

**Failed or absent → register for the supply exam on the KTU portal, pay per subject,
keep your internals, and focus revision on repeated PYQ topics.**

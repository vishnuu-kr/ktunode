Attendance isn't just a formality at KTU — fall below the limit and you may be barred
from the exam. Here's what the rules say and what to do if you're short.

## Minimum attendance

KTU generally requires **at least 75% attendance** in a course to be eligible to sit
the end-semester university exam. This is calculated per the regulations of your scheme.

## What counts as a shortage

- **75% and above** — eligible, no action needed.
- **Between 50% and 75%** — shortage; you may apply for **condonation**.
- **Below 50%** — typically **not condonable**; you may have to repeat the semester.

> Exact thresholds and the number of condonation chances allowed follow the official
> KTU 2024 scheme regulations — always confirm with your college office.

## How condonation works

1. Your college identifies students with a condonable shortage.
2. You submit a **condonation application** with valid reasons (e.g. medical).
3. You pay the **condonation fee**.
4. On approval, you're allowed to write the exam.

## How to avoid the problem

Track your attendance from week one. The [KTUNODE study tools](/tools) include a
timetable to help you plan classes and spot a shortage before it becomes serious.

## Quick answer

**Aim for 75%+.** 50–75% needs paid condonation; below 50% usually means repeating
the semester.

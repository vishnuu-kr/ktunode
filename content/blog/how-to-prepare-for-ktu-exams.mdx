Scoring well in **KTU exams** is less about studying harder and more about
studying in the right order. Here's a proven 7-step plan you can start today.

## Step 1: Get the exact syllabus

Download your subject syllabus and list every module. KTU questions map directly
to modules, so this is your checklist for the whole exam.

## Step 2: Collect module-wise notes

Use clean, [module-wise notes](/notes) instead of dense textbooks. Cover one
module fully before moving to the next.

## Step 3: Solve previous year question papers (PYQs)

KTU repeats question patterns heavily. Solving PYQs shows you which topics carry
the most marks so you can prioritise.

## Step 4: Make one-page module summaries

After each module, condense it to a single page of key formulas, definitions,
and diagrams. This becomes your final-day revision sheet.

## Step 5: Take timed mock attempts

Attempt at least three past papers under exam conditions. This builds speed and
exposes weak areas while you still have time to fix them.

## Step 6: Review mistakes, not just answers

For every wrong or skipped question, note *why* you missed it. Reviewing mistakes
is the single highest-return revision activity.

## Step 7: Final-day light revision

The day before, read only your one-page summaries and formula sheets. Avoid new
topics. Sleep well — recall matters more than cramming.

## Quick answer

If you only have a few days: **read module-wise notes, solve 3 PYQs per subject,
and revise one-page summaries.** That covers the majority of KTU exam marks.

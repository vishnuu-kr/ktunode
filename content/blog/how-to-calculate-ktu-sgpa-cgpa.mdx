Your **SGPA** and **CGPA** decide your KTU degree classification, so it helps to
understand exactly how they're calculated. Here's the formula with a worked example.

## KTU grade points

KTU assigns grade points to letter grades (for example S = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8.5,
and so on, down to the pass grade). Each subject also carries a credit value.

## SGPA formula

> **SGPA = Σ (Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ Credits** (for one semester)

### Worked example

| Subject | Credits | Grade Point | Credit × GP |
|--------|--------:|------------:|------------:|
| Maths  | 4       | 9           | 36          |
| Physics| 3       | 8           | 24          |
| Coding | 3       | 7           | 21          |
| **Total** | **10** |          | **81**      |

SGPA = 81 ÷ 10 = **8.1**

## CGPA formula

> **CGPA = Σ (SGPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Σ Total Credits** (across all semesters)

## CGPA to percentage

KTU commonly uses: **Percentage = (CGPA − 0.5) × 10**. So a CGPA of 8.0 ≈ 75%.

Your KTU result isn't a percentage — it's a **letter grade** that converts to grade
points and then into your SGPA/CGPA. Here's how the system works.

## KTU letter grades and grade points

KTU maps each letter grade to a grade point (highest to lowest):

| Grade | Grade Point | Meaning |
|------|------------:|---------|
| S    | 10 | Outstanding |
| A+   | 9  | Excellent |
| A    | 8.5 | Very good |
| B+   | 8  | Good |
| B    | 7  | Above average |
| C+   | 6.5 | Average |
| C    | 6  | Pass |
| D    | 5.5 | Marginal pass |
| F    | 0  | Fail |

> Exact point values follow your scheme's current regulations — always confirm with
> the official KTU academic regulations for the 2024 scheme.

## Pass marks

To clear a course you typically need a **minimum grade of D**, which means meeting
the minimum internal marks, the minimum external (university exam) marks, **and** the
combined minimum. Missing any one threshold means an F.

## How grades become SGPA / CGPA

Each course's grade point is weighted by its credits to give your **SGPA** per
semester, and the credit-weighted average of all SGPAs gives your **CGPA**. See our
full [SGPA & CGPA calculation guide](/blog/how-to-calculate-ktu-sgpa-cgpa) for the
formula and a worked example, or use the [GPA calculator](/tools?tab=grades).

## Quick answer

**S = 10 points (best), D = minimum pass, F = fail.** Pass each subject by clearing
internal, external, and combined minimums.

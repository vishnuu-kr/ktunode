# DC MACHINES & TRANSFORMERS LAB

## Module 11: Load Test on Single-Phase Transformer

### Topic: Load Test on Single-Phase Transformer (CO4)

This module focuses on understanding and evaluating the performance of a single-phase transformer under varying load conditions. The primary objective is to practically determine key performance parameters and analyze how they change with load.

---

### Learning Outcomes:

Upon successful completion of this experiment, you will be able to:

*   **Determine the equivalent circuit parameters of a single-phase transformer** (referencing CO4).
*   **Calculate the efficiency of the transformer at different load conditions** (referencing CO4).
*   **Calculate the voltage regulation of the transformer at different load conditions** (referencing CO4).
*   **Analyze the effect of load on transformer losses** (referencing CO4).
*   **Plot the efficiency and voltage regulation curves against load percentage** (referencing CO4).

---

### Course Outcome Alignment:

This experiment directly contributes to **CO4: Examine the performance of individual and parallel connected single-phase transformers by conducting load/no-load tests (Knowledge Level: K3)**. By performing the load test, you will gain practical experience in measuring and analyzing the transformer's behavior under different loading scenarios, thereby achieving the K3 (Application) knowledge level.

# DC MACHINES & TRANSFORMERS LAB - Module 10: OC and SC Tests on Single-Phase Transformer

## Topic: Determine the Equivalent Circuit Referred to LV Side and HV Side

### Introduction

This module focuses on understanding the internal parameters of a single-phase transformer by conducting two fundamental tests: the Open-Circuit (OC) test and the Short-Circuit (SC) test. By analyzing the results of these tests, we can derive the approximate equivalent circuit of the transformer, referred to either the Low Voltage (LV) side or the High Voltage (HV) side. This equivalent circuit is crucial for predicting the transformer's behavior under various load conditions and for calculating its efficiency and regulation.

### Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this module, you will be able to:

*   Understand the purpose and procedure of the Open-Circuit (OC) test.
*   Understand the purpose and procedure of the Short-Circuit (SC) test.
*   Determine the shunt branch parameters (magnetizing resistance $R_m$ and leakage reactance $X_m$ or magnetizing reactance $X_\mu$ and core loss resistance $R_c$) from the OC test.
*   Determine the series branch parameters (equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$ and equivalent leakage reactance $X_{eq}$) from the SC test.
*   Refer the equivalent circuit parameters from the LV side to the HV side and vice-versa.
*   Draw the approximate equivalent circuit of a single-phase transformer referred to the LV side.
*   Draw the approximate equivalent circuit of a single-phase transformer referred to the HV side.

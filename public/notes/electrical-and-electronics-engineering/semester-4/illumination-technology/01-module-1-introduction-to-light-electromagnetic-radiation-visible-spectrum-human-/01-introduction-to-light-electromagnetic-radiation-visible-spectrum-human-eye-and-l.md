# ILLUMINATION TECHNOLOGY - Module 1: Introduction to Light

## Topic: Introduction to Light: Electromagnetic radiation, Visible spectrum, Human eye and light perception, Visible light production by black body radiation and emission spectrum, Day lighting, Artificial lighting.

---

### **Module Overview:**

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of light and its interaction with the human visual system. We will explore the nature of light as electromagnetic radiation, understand the visible spectrum and how we perceive light, and delve into the physics behind light production. Furthermore, we will lay the groundwork for understanding different lighting strategies by introducing daylighting and artificial lighting. This module is crucial for building a strong foundation for subsequent topics in illumination engineering.

---

### **Learning Outcomes:**

Upon successful completion of this topic, you will be able to:

*   Describe light as a form of electromagnetic radiation and its properties.
*   Identify and explain the components of the visible light spectrum.
*   Explain the basic principles of human eye operation and light perception.
*   Differentiate between black body radiation and emission spectra as sources of visible light.
*   Define and discuss the principles and benefits of daylighting in architectural design.
*   Define and discuss the fundamental principles and types of artificial lighting.

---

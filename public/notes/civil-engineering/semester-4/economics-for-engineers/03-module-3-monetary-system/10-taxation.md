# ECONOMICS FOR ENGINEERS: Module 3: Monetary System - Taxation

## 1. Introduction to Taxation

Taxation is the compulsory contribution to state revenue, levied by the government on individuals or corporations. It's a fundamental tool for governments to fund public services, infrastructure, and social programs, as well as to influence economic behavior.

### Key Concepts:

*   **Tax Base:** The total value of assets, income, or goods/services that is subject to taxation.
*   **Tax Rate:** The percentage of the tax base that is paid as tax.
*   **Tax Incidence:** Who ultimately bears the burden of a tax, regardless of who legally pays it.
*   **Tax Yield:** The total amount of money raised through taxation.
*   **Tax Evasion:** Illegally avoiding the payment of taxes.
*   **Tax Avoidance:** Legally minimizing tax liability through the use of the tax code.

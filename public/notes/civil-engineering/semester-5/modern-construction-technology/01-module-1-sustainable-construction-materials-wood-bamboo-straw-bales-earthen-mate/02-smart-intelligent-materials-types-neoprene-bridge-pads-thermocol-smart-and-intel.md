### 1. Introduction to Smart & Intelligent Materials in Construction

#### 1.1 Definition of Smart and Intelligent Materials

*   **Smart Materials:** Materials that can respond to external stimuli (like temperature, light, stress, electrical fields, magnetic fields, pH, etc.) in a predictable and reversible way. Their properties change in response to these stimuli.
*   **Intelligent Materials:** A subset of smart materials that not only respond to stimuli but also possess the capability to *process* information and *act* upon that information, often leading to a self-regulating or self-healing behavior. They have a level of autonomy in their response.

#### 1.2 Why Smart & Intelligent Materials in Sustainable Construction?

*   **Enhanced Performance:** Improve structural integrity, durability, and energy efficiency.
*   **Reduced Maintenance:** Self-healing properties can minimize repair needs.
*   **Increased Lifespan:** Components can adapt to changing conditions, extending their useful life.
*   **Energy Efficiency:** Materials that respond to temperature or light can contribute to passive heating/cooling.
*   **Environmental Benefits:** Reduced material usage over time due to longevity and self-repair can lead to a lower environmental footprint.
*   **Safety:** Early detection of damage or structural issues.

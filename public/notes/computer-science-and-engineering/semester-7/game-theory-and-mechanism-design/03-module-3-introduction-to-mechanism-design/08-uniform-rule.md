# Game Theory and Mechanism Design: Module 3 - Introduction to Mechanism Design

## Topic: The Uniform Rule

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of mechanism design, focusing on how to design rules for social choice problems to achieve desirable outcomes. The Uniform Rule is a specific mechanism designed to allocate a single indivisible good among multiple agents with different valuations.

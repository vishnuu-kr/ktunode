# Digital Forensics: Module 3: Mobile Forensics

## Topic: Analysis of Application Files - Social Media Files

This module delves into the intricacies of recovering and analyzing data from mobile applications, with a specific focus on social media platforms. Understanding how data is stored and accessed on mobile devices is crucial for extracting valuable evidence from these ubiquitous applications.

### Learning Outcomes:

By the end of this topic, you should be able to:

1.  **Understand the purpose and importance of analyzing social media files in digital forensics.**
2.  **Identify common types of social media files and their typical storage locations on mobile devices.**
3.  **Describe various methods and techniques for acquiring and analyzing social media data.**
4.  **Recognize the challenges and considerations associated with social media forensics.**
5.  **Interpret and report findings from social media data analysis.**

---

### 1. Purpose and Importance of Analyzing Social Media Files

Social media platforms are integral to modern communication and have become a significant source of digital evidence in investigations. Analyzing social media files allows forensic examiners to:

*   **Establish Communication Patterns:** Track interactions between individuals, identify contacts, and understand the nature of relationships

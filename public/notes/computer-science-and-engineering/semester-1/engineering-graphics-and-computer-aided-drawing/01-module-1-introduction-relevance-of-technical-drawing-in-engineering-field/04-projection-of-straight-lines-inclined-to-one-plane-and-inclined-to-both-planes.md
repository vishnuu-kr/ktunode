# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS AND COMPUTER AIDED DRAWING

## Module 1: Introduction: Relevance of Technical Drawing in Engineering Field

### Topic: Projection of Straight Lines: Inclined to One Plane and Inclined to Both Planes

**(Connecting to Course Outcomes: CO1, CO2)**

Welcome, everyone! Today, we're going to build upon our understanding of points and start tackling a more complex but equally vital element: **straight lines**. Why are lines so important? Think about it: any complex engineering object – a bridge, a car engine, a smartphone – is ultimately made up of many interconnected lines and curves. Being able to accurately represent these lines, especially when they aren't perfectly horizontal or vertical, is the bedrock of technical drawing. This is directly linked to **CO1: Understand the projection of points and lines located in different quadrants**, as we’re now extending our knowledge from single points to lines. Furthermore, mastering this skill is essential for **CO2: Prepare multiview orthographic projections of objects by visualizing them in different positions**, because once we can project lines, we can project the edges and contours of even the most intricate shapes.

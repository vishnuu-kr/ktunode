# ADVANCED NUMERICAL CONTROL IN MANUFACTURING

## Module 4: Computer Aided Part Programming

### Topic: Computer Aided Part Programming

---

**Introduction:**

This module delves into the evolution of part programming, moving from manual methods to the sophisticated realm of Computer-Aided Part Programming (CAPP). CAPP significantly streamlines the process of generating machine instructions for CNC machines, enhancing efficiency, accuracy, and complexity of manufactured parts. We will explore the fundamental concepts, tools, and benefits of CAPP, with a strong emphasis on practical application and alignment with our course outcomes.

---

### Learning Outcomes:

By the end of this topic, you will be able to:

*   **Understand the principles of Computer-Aided Part Programming (CAPP).** (K2)
*   **Differentiate between manual and computer-aided part programming.** (K2)
*   **Identify the various software tools and technologies used in CAPP.** (K2)
*   **Explain the steps involved in creating a CAPP-generated part program.** (K2)
*   **Recognize the benefits and limitations of CAPP.** (K2)
*   **Appreciate how CAPP contributes to the overall manufacturing process.** (K2)

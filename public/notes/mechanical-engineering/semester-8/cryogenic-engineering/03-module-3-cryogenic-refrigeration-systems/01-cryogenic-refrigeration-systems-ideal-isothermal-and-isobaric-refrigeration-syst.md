# Cryogenic Engineering: Module 3 - Cryogenic Refrigeration Systems

## Topic: Ideal Isothermal and Isobaric Refrigeration Systems

### 1. Introduction to Cryogenic Refrigeration Systems

Cryogenic refrigeration systems are designed to achieve and maintain very low temperatures, typically below -150°C (123 K). These systems are crucial for a wide range of applications, including superconducting magnets in MRI machines, scientific research, liquefaction of gases like helium and hydrogen, and food preservation

# Engineering Ethics and Sustainable Development - Module 3: Hydrology and Water Management

## Topic: Circular Economy and Degrowth: Introduction to the Circular Economy Model

This module explores the fundamental principles of hydrology and the water cycle, providing a crucial foundation for understanding how water resources are managed sustainably. Within this context, we introduce the concept of the Circular Economy and its relationship to Degrowth as critical approaches to address environmental challenges and promote sustainable development.

## Topic: Design of Outdoor Lighting: Street Lighting

This module focuses on the principles and practices involved in designing effective and efficient street lighting systems. We will explore the types of streets, their illumination requirements, essential terminology, fixture choices, arrangement strategies, desired qualities of good street lighting, lamp and luminaire selection, and methods for calculating illumination levels.

---

### 1. Types of Streets and Their Required Illumination Levels

Street lighting is crucial for public safety, security, and improving the overall urban environment. The required illumination levels vary significantly based on the type of street, traffic volume, pedestrian activity, and the surrounding environment. Standards like the National Lighting Code (SP72:2010) and international guidelines provide recommendations.

**Key Concepts:**

*   **Illuminance (E):** The amount of light falling on a surface, measured in lux (lx) or footcandles (fc).
*   **Average Luminance (L):** The brightness of the road surface, perceived by the observer, measured in candelas per square meter (cd/m²). This is a more critical parameter for road safety than illuminance itself, as it relates to how well drivers can see objects.
*   **Uniformity:** The degree to which the illuminance or luminance is evenly distributed across the road surface. High uniformity reduces distracting dark areas.

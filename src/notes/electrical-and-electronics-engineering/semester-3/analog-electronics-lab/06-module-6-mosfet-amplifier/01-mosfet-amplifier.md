# ANALOG ELECTRONICS LAB - Module 6: MOSFET Amplifier

## Topic: MOSFET Amplifier

### Introduction

This module delves into the practical implementation and analysis of MOSFET amplifiers, a fundamental building block in modern analog electronic circuits. MOSFETs (Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistors) offer advantages such as high input impedance, low power consumption, and scalability, making them ideal for amplifier designs. This module aims to equip you with the knowledge and skills to design, build, analyze, and simulate MOSFET amplifier circuits.

### Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this module, you will be able to:

*   **LO1:** Identify the different types of MOSFETs and their basic operating principles.
*   **LO2:** Analyze the DC biasing of MOSFET amplifier circuits.
*   **LO3:** Design common MOSFET amplifier configurations (Common Source, Common Drain, Common Gate).
*   **LO4:** Analyze the AC performance of MOSFET amplifiers, including voltage gain, input impedance, and output impedance.
*   **LO5:** Understand the effect of coupling and bypass capacitors on amplifier performance.
*   **LO6:** Utilize laboratory equipment and simulation tools to verify the performance of MOSFET amplifiers.

This module directly contributes to the following course outcomes:

*   **CO1 (K1):** You will use various electronic instruments (oscilloscope, function generator, multimeter) to conduct experiments on MOSFET amplifiers.

*   **CO6 (K2):** You will gain practical experience in building and testing circuits, which implicitly involves understanding component placement and circuit layout principles.

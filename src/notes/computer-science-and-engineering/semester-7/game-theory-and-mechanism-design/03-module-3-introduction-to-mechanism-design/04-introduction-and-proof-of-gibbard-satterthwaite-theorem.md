# Module 3: Introduction to Mechanism Design

## Topic: Introduction and Proof of the Gibbard-Satterthwaite Theorem

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of mechanism design, focusing on the seminal Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem. We will explore what mechanism design is, its core objectives, and then delve into the proof of this crucial theorem, which has profound implications for the design of voting systems and other social choice mechanisms.

---

### Learning Outcomes:

Upon successful completion of this topic, you will be able to:

*   Understand the core problem mechanism design aims to solve.
*   Define key terms such as mechanism, outcome, preference, strategy-proofness, and dictatorship.
*   Explain the concept of social choice functions and their limitations.
*   State and understand the Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem.
*   Outline the general approach to proving the Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem.
*   Demonstrate understanding of the implications of the theorem for designing fair and desirable mechanisms.

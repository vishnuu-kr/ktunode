# Module 3: Introduction to Mechanism Design - Task Sharing Domain

## Introduction to Mechanism Design

Mechanism design is the study of how to design the rules of a game (the "mechanism") to achieve a desired outcome, given that players (agents) will act strategically based on their own private information. It's about designing the *rules of the game* rather than playing a game that already exists.

### Key Concepts:

*   **Agents:** Individuals or entities participating in the mechanism. They have private information (e.g., preferences, costs, capabilities).

# Module 3: Introduction to Mechanism Design - Examples of Quasi-Linear Preferences

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

*   Understand the concept of quasi-linear preferences.
*   Identify and explain common examples of quasi-linear preferences in economic settings.
*   Analyze the implications of quasi-linear preferences for mechanism design.

# Digital Forensics: Module 3 - Mobile Forensics

## Topic: Challenges in Mobile Forensics

---

### 1. Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this topic, you should be able to:

*   **Identify and explain the common challenges encountered in mobile forensic investigations.**
*   **Discuss the impact of evolving mobile technologies on forensic methodologies.**
*   **Analyze the difficulties associated with data acquisition and preservation from mobile devices.**
*   **Evaluate the complexities of data interpretation and analysis in mobile forensics.**
*   **Understand the legal and ethical considerations specific to mobile device investigations.**
*   **Recognize the role of encryption and anti-forensic techniques in hindering investigations.**
*   **Explore strategies and tools used to overcome or mitigate common mobile forensic challenges.**

---

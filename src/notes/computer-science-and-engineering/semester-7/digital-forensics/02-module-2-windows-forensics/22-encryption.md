# Digital Forensics: Module 2 - Windows Forensics

## Topic: Encryption

This module explores the impact of encryption on Windows digital forensics investigations. We will delve into the types of encryption commonly found on Windows systems, the challenges they present to investigators, and the techniques used to overcome them.

---

### Learning Outcomes:

*   **Understand the role of encryption in Windows operating systems.**
*   **Identify common encryption technologies and their implementations in Windows.**
*   **Analyze the impact of encryption on evidence acquisition and analysis.**
*   **Explore techniques and tools for decrypting or bypassing encryption during forensic investigations.**
*   **Discuss legal and ethical considerations related to accessing encrypted data.**

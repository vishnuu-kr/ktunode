### 1. Introduction to Contact Mechanics in Tribology

*   **Tribology** is the science and engineering of interacting surfaces in relative motion. It includes the study and application of the principles of friction, wear, and lubrication.
*   **Contact** is the fundamental interaction between surfaces in relative motion. Understanding the nature of this contact is crucial for predicting friction, wear, and the effectiveness of lubrication.

# NANOTECHNOLOGY: Module 4 - Nanomaterials for Energy and Environment: Electrochemical Energy Storage

## Topic: Lab-on-Chips and Integrated Systems

This module delves into the exciting intersection of nanotechnology, energy storage, and environmental applications. Specifically, this topic focuses on the development and application of "lab-on-chips" (LOCs) and integrated systems for electrochemical energy storage, leveraging the unique properties of nanomaterials.

---

### **1. Introduction to Lab-on-Chips (LOCs)**

#### **1.1 Definition and Concept**

*   **Lab-on-a-Chip (LOC):** A miniaturized device that integrates multiple laboratory functions (e.g., sample preparation, chemical reactions, separation, detection) onto a single substrate, often a microfluidic chip.
*   **Miniaturization:** Shrinks complex analytical procedures from bench-scale laboratories to a few square centimeters or even millimeters.
*   **Microfluidics:** The science and technology of manipulating and controlling fluids in the micrometer scale (typically 1-1000 µm). This forms the backbone of LOC devices.
*   **Key Advantage:** Enables faster analysis, reduced reagent consumption, lower power requirements, and potential for portable, point-of-care applications.

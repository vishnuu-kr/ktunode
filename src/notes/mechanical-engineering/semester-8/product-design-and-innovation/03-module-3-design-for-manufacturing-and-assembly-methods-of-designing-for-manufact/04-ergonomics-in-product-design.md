---

### 1. Introduction to Ergonomics

**Definition:** Ergonomics is the scientific study of people at work. The objective is to design the job, equipment, and workplace to fit the worker. By designing for usability, we reduce human error, increase productivity, and improve safety.

**Key Concepts:**
*   **Human Factors:** The study of how humans interact with systems, considering their physical, cognitive, and perceptual capabilities and limitations.
*   **Usability:** The ease with which users can use a product to achieve their goals.
*   **Comfort:** The absence of physical strain or discomfort during product use.
*   **Safety:** Designing products to minimize the risk of injury or harm to users.
*   **Efficiency:** Designing products that allow users to perform tasks quickly and effectively.

**Alignment with Course Outcomes:**
*   **CO5: Incorporate ergonomics and rapid prototyping in product development (Knowledge Level: K2):** This topic directly addresses the incorporation of ergonomics into the product development process. Understanding ergonomic principles is crucial for creating user-centered designs.

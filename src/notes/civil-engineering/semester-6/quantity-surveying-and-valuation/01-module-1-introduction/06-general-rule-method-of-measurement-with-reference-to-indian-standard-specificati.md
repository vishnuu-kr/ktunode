# Quantity Surveying and Valuation: Module 1 - Introduction

## Topic: General Rule & Method of Measurement (IS 1200)

This module introduces the fundamental principles of quantity surveying, focusing on the standardized methods for measuring building works as per the Indian Standard Specification IS 1200. Understanding these rules is crucial for accurate cost estimation, tendering, and contract administration.

---

### Learning Outcomes:

Upon successful completion of this topic, you will be able to:

1.  **Understand the purpose and importance of IS 1200.**
2.  **Explain the general rules of measurement for building works.**
3.  **Describe the common methods of measurement for various building components.**
4.  **Identify the units of measurement for different quantities.**
5.  **Apply IS 1200 principles to abstract and bill of quantities preparation.**

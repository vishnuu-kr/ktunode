# Engineering Geology: Module 4 - Structural Geology: Attitude of Rocks - Dip and Strike

## Topic: Brief Classification and Engineering Significance of Folds

### Learning Outcomes:

*   Understand the fundamental concepts of folds in rock formations.
*   Identify and classify different types of folds.
*   Explain the engineering significance of folds in geological contexts.
*   Relate the orientation and geometry of folds to potential engineering challenges and opportunities.

---

### 1. Introduction to Folds

**Definition:** Folds are wave-like undulations or bends in rock layers that result from compressional forces acting on the Earth's crust over geological time. These forces cause the originally flat-lying strata to deform plastically.

**Key Concepts:**

*   **Stress and Strain:** Folds are a direct result of **stress** (force applied per unit area) exceeding the rock's **yield strength**, leading to **strain** (deformation).
*   **Plastic Deformation:** Rocks deform plastically when subjected to sufficient stress and temperature, allowing them to bend rather than fracture.
*   **Compressional Forces:** Folds are typically formed under **compressional tectonic regimes**, where tectonic plates are pushing against each other.

**Anatomy of a Fold:**

*   **Hinge:** The point of maximum curvature in a fold.
*   **Limb:** The relatively flat or gently dipping sections on either side of the hinge.
*   **Axial Plane (or Symmetry Plane):** An imaginary plane that bisects the angle between the two limbs. In symmetrical folds, this plane is perpendicular to the bedding planes.
*   **Crest:** The highest point of an anticline.
*   **Trough:** The lowest point of a syncline.
*   **Plunge:** The angle that the axis of a fold makes with the horizontal. If the axis is horizontal, the fold is **plunge-less**.

---

### 2. Classification of Folds

Folds are classified based on several criteria:

**A. Based on Shape and Geometry:**

*   **Symmetrical Folds:** The axial plane bisects the acute angle between the limbs, and the limbs dip at equal angles but in opposite directions.
    *   **Anticline:** A fold that arches upwards, with the oldest rocks in the center. The limbs dip away from the crest.
    *   **Syncline:** A fold that arches downwards, with the youngest rocks in the center. The limbs dip towards the trough.

## Topic: Break-even Analysis

---

### **1. Introduction to Break-even Analysis**

Break-even analysis is a crucial tool in managerial economics and business decision-making. It helps determine the point at which total revenue equals total costs, meaning there is neither profit nor loss. Understanding this point is vital for pricing, production planning, cost control, and investment decisions.

### **3. Key Concepts and Definitions**

#### **3.1. Costs**

*   **Fixed Costs (FC):** Costs that do not change with the level of output or sales within a relevant range. These are incurred even if no units are produced or sold.
    *   **Examples:** Rent, salaries of administrative staff, insurance, depreciation of fixed assets, property taxes.
*   **Variable Costs (VC):** Costs that vary directly and proportionally with the level of output or sales.
    *   **Examples:** Direct materials, direct labor, sales commissions, packaging costs, shipping costs per unit.
*   **Total Costs (TC):** The sum of fixed costs and total variable costs.
    *   **Formula:** TC = FC + TVC
    *   **Where:** TVC = Variable Cost per Unit (VCU) × Quantity (Q)
    *   **Therefore:** TC = FC + (VCU × Q)

#### **3.2. Revenue**

*   **Sales Revenue (SR) / Total Revenue (TR):** The total income generated from selling a product or service.
    *   **Formula:** SR = Selling Price per Unit (SPU) × Quantity (Q)

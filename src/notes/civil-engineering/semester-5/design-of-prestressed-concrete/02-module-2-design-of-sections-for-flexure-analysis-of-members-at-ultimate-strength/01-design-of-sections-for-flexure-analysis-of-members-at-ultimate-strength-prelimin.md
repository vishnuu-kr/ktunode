# DESIGN OF PRESTRESSED CONCRETE - Module 2: Design of Sections for Flexure: Analysis of Members at Ultimate Strength

## Topic: Preliminary Design & Final Design for Type 1 Members

### Learning Outcomes:

Upon completion of this module, you should be able to:

*   Understand the concept of ultimate flexural strength of prestressed concrete members.
*   Identify different failure modes of prestressed concrete sections at ultimate load.
*   Perform preliminary design calculations for prestressed concrete sections to achieve a desired ultimate flexural strength.
*   Conduct final design calculations for Type 1 members (members where the concrete in the compression zone is stressed to its maximum capacity at ultimate load) subjected to flexure.
*   Apply relevant design codes (e.g., IS 1343) for the analysis and design of prestressed concrete sections at ultimate strength.

## 1. Introduction to Ultimate Flexural Strength

The ultimate flexural strength (or moment capacity, $M_{ult}$) of a prestressed concrete member is the maximum bending moment the section can resist before failure. At this stage, the concrete in the compression zone reaches its ultimate compressive strength, and the steel reinforcement (prestressed tendons and/or non-prestressed reinforcement) yields or fractures.

### Key Concepts:

*   **Ultimate Limit State:** The state at which a structure or a part of a structure ceases to perform its intended function due to excessive deformation, collapse, or other forms of failure.
*   **Flexural Failure:** Failure occurring due to bending.
*   **Strain Compatibility:** The principle that strains are continuous

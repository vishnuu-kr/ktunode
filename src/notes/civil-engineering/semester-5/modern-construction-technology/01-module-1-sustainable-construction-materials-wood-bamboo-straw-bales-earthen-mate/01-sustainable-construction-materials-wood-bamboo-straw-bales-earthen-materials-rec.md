# MODERN CONSTRUCTION TECHNOLOGY: MODULE 1 - SUSTAINABLE CONSTRUCTION MATERIALS

## 1. Introduction to Sustainable Construction Materials

**Definition:** Sustainable construction materials are those that minimize environmental impact throughout their lifecycle, from extraction/production to disposal. They prioritize resource efficiency, reduced pollution, energy conservation, and human health.

**Key Concepts:**

*   **Lifecycle Assessment (LCA):** A comprehensive evaluation of the environmental impacts of a material throughout its entire lifespan (extraction, manufacturing, transportation, use, and disposal/recycling).
*   **Embodied Energy:** The total energy consumed in the production and transportation of a building material.
*   **Renewable Resources:** Materials that can be replenished naturally at a rate comparable to or faster than their consumption.
*   **Recyclability/Reusability:** Materials that can be processed and reused in new products or applications

The **KTU 2024 scheme** is the latest B.Tech curriculum from APJ Abdul Kalam
Technological University. If you joined in the 2024 batch, this is the structure
your entire degree follows. Here's what actually changed and what it means for you.

## What is the KTU 2024 scheme?

The 2024 scheme is a revised credit-based curriculum effective from the 2024
admission year. It reorganises courses into clearer baskets, adds flexible
**honours and minor** pathways, and adjusts how internal and external marks are
weighted. The goal is a more flexible, outcome-based degree.

## Key changes at a glance

- **Credit structure:** ~160 total credits across 8 semesters.
- **Honours & minors:** eligible students can earn an honours degree or a minor
  in another discipline by taking extra credits.
- **Continuous assessment:** more weight on internal evaluation and lab/project work.
- **Course baskets:** clearer grouping into basic sciences, engineering sciences,
  program core, electives, and humanities.

## What this means for your exam prep

Because internal assessment carries more weight, consistency matters more than
last-minute cramming. Use [module-wise notes](/notes) to stay on top of each
unit, and track your syllabus so nothing slips before internals.

## How to plan your semester

Start every semester by mapping your subjects to their modules, then schedule
revision around internal assessment dates. The [KTUNODE study tools](/tools)
include a timetable and GPA calculator to help you plan.

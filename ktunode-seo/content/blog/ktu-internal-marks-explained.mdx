Under the KTU **2024 scheme**, internal marks carry more weight than ever — they can be
the difference between a pass and a fail. Here's how they work and how to max them out.

## What makes up internal marks

KTU **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)** typically combines:

- **Internal test / series exams** (the largest chunk)
- **Assignments**
- **Tutorials / attendance / other components** as defined by the scheme

The exact split is set by the **2024 scheme regulations** — confirm with your department.

## Why they matter so much

Your final course mark = **internal marks + university (external) exam marks.** Strong
internals give you a cushion: you need fewer external marks to pass and to push into a
higher grade band. Weak internals put all the pressure on the final exam.

## How to score full internals

1. **Never skip series exams** — they're the biggest component. Prep with
   [module-wise notes](/notes) and PYQs.
2. **Submit every assignment on time** — easy marks people lose to deadlines.
3. **Protect your attendance** — it often feeds internal marks and exam eligibility.
4. **Be consistent** — internals reward steady work, not last-minute cramming.

## Quick answer

**Internal marks = series exams + assignments + attendance/tutorials,** added to your
university exam total. They're retained for supply exams, so earning them once pays off
twice.
